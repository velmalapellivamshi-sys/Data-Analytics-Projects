# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis – Excel Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail sales dataset using **Microsoft Excel**.

The workbook contains **1,000 retail sales records** covering the year **2025**. It includes sales, product, regional, and customer satisfaction information.

The project uses Excel-based analysis and visualizations to understand revenue performance across regions, product categories, products, and months.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total retail revenue
- Analyze units sold
- Compare revenue across regions
- Compare revenue across product categories
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Analyze customer satisfaction
- Present findings using Excel charts and summary tables

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Excel Pivot Tables

## 📁 Dataset

The main dataset contains the following fields:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| OrderID | Unique order identifier |
| OrderDate | Date of the order |
| Region | Sales region |
| Category | Product category |
| Product | Product name |
| UnitsSold | Number of units sold |
| UnitPrice | Price per unit |
| Revenue | Revenue generated from the order |
| CustomerSatisfaction | Customer satisfaction rating |

### Dataset Summary

- **Records:** 1,000
- **Period:** January 2025 – December 2025
- **Regions:** East, North, South, West
- **Categories:** Furniture, Electronics, Office Supplies
- **Products:** 9

## 📈 Analysis Included

### 1. Revenue by Region

Revenue is analyzed across four regions:

- East
- North
- South
- West

**South** generated the highest revenue at **2,910,054.10**.

### 2. Revenue by Category

The project compares three product categories:

- Electronics
- Office Supplies
- Furniture

**Electronics** generated the highest category revenue at **3,633,267.97**.

### 3. Revenue by Product

Revenue is also analyzed at the individual product level.

The highest-revenue products include:

- Laptop – 1,291,249.01
- Pen – 1,268,985.20
- Tablet – 1,262,282.58

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trend

Monthly revenue is analyzed from January through December 2025.

The highest monthly revenue was recorded in **May: 1,087,347.63**.

The lowest monthly revenue was recorded in **December: 721,308.61**.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction

The dataset contains customer satisfaction ratings for each order.

The overall average customer satisfaction is approximately **4.01**.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dataset:

- Total revenue was **10.32 million**.
- A total of **13,321 units** were sold.
- The **South** region generated the highest revenue among the four regions.
- **Electronics** was the highest-revenue product category.
- **Laptop** generated the highest revenue among the individual products.
- **May** was the strongest month by revenue.
- **December** was the lowest month by revenue.
- The average customer satisfaction score was approximately **4.01 out of 5**.
